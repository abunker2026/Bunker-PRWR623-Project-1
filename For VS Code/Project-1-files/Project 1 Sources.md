# Sources and Acknowledgments
## Publicly available information
The procedure directly or indirectly references the following sources of publication and grant information commonly utilized by biomedical researchers and research administrators of biomedical research.

* PubMed, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/. This is a federally funded database that provides the public with access to over 40 million publications, abstracts, etc. focusing primarily on the biomedicine, life science, and related fields.
* Dimensions, https://www.dimensions.ai/. Not mentioned directly in the procedure, Dimensions is a database built on using AI tools to link publicly available research data (e.g., grant application abstracts, publications, published patent applications, etc.) and facilitate answering myriad research questions. Therefore, it *could* be a tool utilized by research administrators to determine the relatedness of a publication to a grant.
* Pre-print definition, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/about/nihpreprints/. The background information explaining pre-print publications was adapted from the National Library of Medicine's (NLM's) webpage about the topic.


## Not publicly available information
The premise of the procedure is based off the author's experiences working for a federal organization that funds biomedical research. Some of the tools and systems mentioned in the procedure are only accessible to federal employees.

* The screenshot of the publication repository data-capture interface is from the electronic grants management system, called EGS, used by the author's organization. No system data are compromised through the use of the screen shot.
* The definitions used throughout the documentation come from the author's experience as a research administrator and evaluator; there are no formal sources for the definitions, other when explicitly stated otherwise.
